# Korea Convenience Store Franchise Comparison

## Project
A responsive bilingual English/Korean single-page website comparing CU, GS25, and 7-Eleven Korea by reported store count.

## Metric and reported values
- CU: 18,255
- GS25: 17,989
- 7-Eleven Korea: 12,081
- Stated base year: 2024
- Unit: stores in South Korea

## Important data note
The brand-level figures come from a secondary comparison source and have not been independently verified against each brand's original franchise disclosure. Confirm whether each figure means total stores or franchise-only stores before relying on the ranking. Do not treat these as official verified figures, or as a measure of revenue or profit.

## Sources
1. Secondary reported store-count comparison: https://francha.craftspace.co.kr/ranking/stores/convenience
2. Korea Fair Trade Commission, 2024 franchise business status statistics: https://www.ftc.go.kr/www/selectBbsNttView.do?bordCd=3&key=12&nttSn=45987&pageIndex=1&pageUnit=10&searchCnd=all&searchCtgry=01%2C02
3. Korea Policy Briefing, KFTC release: https://m.korea.kr/briefing/pressReleaseView.do?newsId=156683272
4. Statistics Korea / National Data Office, 2024 franchise survey: https://www.kostat.go.kr/boardDownload.es?bid=11771&list_no=443742&seq=2
5. Official franchise information system: https://franchise.ftc.go.kr/

## Features
- English and Korean descriptions
- Mobile-responsive layout
- Table sorting from highest to lowest and lowest to highest
- Source links and verification caveats
- No external APIs or frameworks

## Before submission
Check the original disclosure documents for all three brands, verify the base year and store definition, and test the source links. If the figures do not share the same definition, do not rank them together.
